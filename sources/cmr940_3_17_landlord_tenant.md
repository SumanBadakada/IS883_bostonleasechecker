# 940 CMR 3.17: Attorney General's landlord-tenant regulations (Consumer Protection)

> Permission: Code of Massachusetts Regulations are public government publications.
> These regulations make the listed practices "unfair or deceptive" under M.G.L. c. 93A.

## cmr-3.17-1 | 940 CMR 3.17(1)
Conditions and maintenance. It is an unfair practice for a landlord to rent an apartment that has
conditions violating the State Sanitary Code, or to fail to fix such conditions within a reasonable
time after notice. A lease clause making the tenant responsible for repairs the landlord is legally
required to make (heat, hot water, structural, plumbing, electrical, pest problems not caused by the
tenant) is inconsistent with these duties.

## cmr-3.17-2 | 940 CMR 3.17(2)
Disclosure of identity. The landlord must disclose to the tenant, in writing, the name, address and
telephone number of the owner and of any person authorised to manage the property and to receive
notices and legal papers on the owner's behalf. A lease that does not identify who to contact for
repairs and notices fails this requirement.

## cmr-3.17-3a | 940 CMR 3.17(3)(a)
Lease terms that are not allowed. A landlord may not include in a lease any term that violates a law,
or that waives the tenant's rights or the landlord's legal duties. Examples treated as unfair include
terms that: make the tenant pay the landlord's attorney's fees beyond what the law allows; release the
landlord from liability for the landlord's own negligence; waive the tenant's right to a habitable
apartment; require the tenant to pay for repairs not caused by the tenant; or penalise the tenant for
exercising legal rights such as complaining to a code-enforcement agency.

## cmr-3.17-3b | 940 CMR 3.17(3)(b)
Copy of the lease. The landlord must give the tenant a fully signed copy of any written lease within
thirty days after the tenant signs it.

## cmr-3.17-4 | 940 CMR 3.17(4)
Security deposits and rent in advance. Failing to follow the security deposit and last month's rent
rules of M.G.L. c. 186 §15B (amounts allowed at move-in, separate account, receipts, interest, the
statement of condition, and return within thirty days) is an unfair or deceptive practice.

## cmr-3.17-5 | 940 CMR 3.17(5)
Termination and retaliation. A landlord may not threaten or take action to end a tenancy, raise the
rent, or change the terms of a tenancy because the tenant reported code violations, complained to the
landlord or a government agency, or joined a tenants' organisation. Lease terms that let the landlord
end the tenancy without going through the court eviction process (for example by changing the locks or
removing belongings) are not allowed.

## cmr-3.17-6 | 940 CMR 3.17(6)
Other practices. A landlord may not misrepresent the tenant's rights, enforce a lease term the landlord
knows is void, or charge fees the law does not permit.
