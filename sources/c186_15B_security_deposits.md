# M.G.L. c. 186 §15B: Security deposits, last month's rent, entry, late fees

## c186-15B-1a | M.G.L. c. 186 §15B(1)(a)
Landlord entry. A lease may let the landlord enter the apartment only to inspect it, make repairs,
show it to a prospective tenant, buyer, mortgagee or their agent, in accordance with a court order,
or if the tenant appears to have abandoned the apartment. A lease clause that gives the landlord a
broader right to enter at any time for any reason goes beyond what the statute allows.

## c186-15B-1b | M.G.L. c. 186 §15B(1)(b)
Money at the start of a tenancy. At or before the start of a tenancy a landlord may require only:
(i) rent for the first full month; (ii) rent for the last month, calculated at the same rate as the
first month; (iii) a security deposit no greater than the first month's rent; and (iv) the purchase and
installation cost of a new lock and key. A landlord may not require any other payment, fee or charge at
the start of the tenancy, such as a pet fee, an application fee, a move-in fee, a cleaning fee or a
key fee beyond the lock cost. A pet deposit counts as part of the security deposit and so falls within
the one-month cap.

## c186-15B-1c | M.G.L. c. 186 §15B(1)(c)
Late fees. No lease or rental agreement may impose interest or a penalty for failure to pay rent until
thirty days after the rent was due. A late fee charged after 5, 10 or 15 days is therefore not allowed.

## c186-15B-2a | M.G.L. c. 186 §15B(2)(a)
Last month's rent. A landlord who collects last month's rent in advance must give the tenant a receipt
stating the amount, the date, the name of the person receiving it, and that the tenant is entitled to
interest on it. The landlord must pay interest every year at 5 percent, or the rate the bank pays if
the money is in a bank account, or allow the tenant to deduct it from rent.

## c186-15B-2b | M.G.L. c. 186 §15B(2)(b)
Security deposit receipt. When a landlord receives a security deposit, the landlord must give the
tenant a written receipt with the amount, the date, a description of the apartment, and the name of
the person receiving it and the landlord's name, signed by the person receiving it.

## c186-15B-2c | M.G.L. c. 186 §15B(2)(c)
Statement of condition. Within ten days after the start of the tenancy or receipt of the security
deposit, whichever is later, the landlord must give the tenant a separate written statement of the
present condition of the apartment, listing any damage. The tenant has fifteen days to return it with
any additions. A lease that says the tenant accepts the apartment "as is" does not remove this duty.

## c186-15B-3a | M.G.L. c. 186 §15B(3)(a)
Holding the deposit. The security deposit must be held in a separate, interest-bearing account in a
bank located in Massachusetts, protected from the landlord's creditors, and remains the tenant's
property. Within thirty days the landlord must give the tenant a receipt naming the bank, its address,
the account number and the amount deposited. A deposit may not be mixed with the landlord's own money.

## c186-15B-3b | M.G.L. c. 186 §15B(3)(b)
Interest on the deposit. The tenant earns interest on the security deposit, paid at the end of each
year of the tenancy, at 5 percent or the rate the bank pays. A lease cannot say the tenant gives up
this interest.

## c186-15B-4 | M.G.L. c. 186 §15B(4)
Returning the deposit. Within thirty days after the tenancy ends, the landlord must return the deposit
with interest, minus only: (i) unpaid rent; (ii) a real-estate tax increase the lease validly passes on;
and (iii) the reasonable cost of repairing damage beyond reasonable wear and tear, listed in an itemised
statement signed under the pains and penalties of perjury, with written evidence such as bills or
estimates. A lease may not let the landlord keep the deposit for other reasons, such as a flat
cleaning charge, carpet cleaning regardless of condition, or "liquidated damages".

## c186-15B-6 | M.G.L. c. 186 §15B(6)
Losing the right to keep the deposit. A landlord who does not hold the deposit in a separate account,
does not give the required receipts or statement of condition, does not provide the itemised list of
damage, or uses the deposit in violation of the statute, must return the whole deposit with interest.

## c186-15B-7 | M.G.L. c. 186 §15B(7)
Penalties. A landlord who fails to return a deposit as required may owe the tenant three times the
amount of the deposit or balance due, plus interest, court costs and reasonable attorney's fees.

## c186-15B-8 | M.G.L. c. 186 §15B(8)
No waiver. Any lease provision that conflicts with this section, or that tries to make the tenant give
up any right under it, is void and cannot be enforced.
